Hi, I'm Murat!

I am a Senior Perception Engineer at, RSquared Robotics a division of Ranpak where I design, build, and deploy computer vision and machine learning systems for real-time industrial automation. My work spans sensor fusion, 3D reconstruction, deep learning acceleration, and production-grade inference systems.

I completed my Ph.D. in Computer Engineering at Virginia Tech under Paul Plassmann and Creed F. Jones. My doctoral work focused on robot perception, scalable multi-sensor fusion, computational modeling, and high-fidelity 3D reconstruction in complex environments. My work also included large-scale mapping from aerial images.

Prior to my doctoral studies, I earned my M.S. in Mechanical Engineering from Virginia Tech under Tomonari Furukawa, where I developed a wireless propagation model and an indoor localization technique tailored for challenging non-line-of-sight environments.

I received my B.Sc. in Mechatronics Engineering from Kocaeli University, Turkey. My early work spanned embedded systems, robotics, machine vision, and automated sensing platforms.

My technical interests include Robot Perception, Real-Time 3D Vision, Gaussian Splatting, Deep Learning Acceleration, Localization & Mapping, and Large-Scale Computer Vision Systems. I enjoy building end-to-end systems that bridge theory, engineering, and deployment.

Research and Projects

Research Interests

My research spans the intersection of robot perception, 3D computer vision, and efficient deep learning systems. I focus on building scalable, real-time pipelines for autonomous perception, mapping, and decision-making. My interests include:

  • Robot Perception & Sensor Fusion – real-time integration of RGB, depth, multi-view imagery, inertial, and geometric cues for robust environmental understanding.
  • 3D Reconstruction & Mapping – large-scale Gaussian Splatting, multi-view stereo (MVS), TSDF-based fusion, voxel hashing, and city-scale map generation.
  • Deep Learning Acceleration – TensorRT-based deployment, CUDA kernels, quantization, distillation (DINOv2 → YOLO), and low-latency inference for edge devices and embedded platforms.
  • Localization & Pose Estimation – multi-camera geometry, PnP solvers, visual odometry, and 6DoF estimation in constrained or imperfect calibration settings.
  • Industrial Vision Systems – high-throughput inspection, segmentation, tracking, object detection, and multi-camera redundancy for safety-critical robotics.
  • Machine Learning for Physical Systems – bridging geometric reasoning with learning-based models for robust performance in real environments.

Across all areas, I enjoy developing end-to-end systems—from algorithm design to optimized deployment—that remain stable, interpretable, and production-ready at scale.
